We conducted an open beta test for our decision aid, which concluded on Monday, April 22, 2024.

These are the three papers produced based on this project, which was funded by a Maastricht University Diversity & Inclusivity (D&I) Grant:

📄 Opening up? Exploring motives and needs of students and staff of a Dutch university on disclosing mental health issues to inform decision aid development (Published in PLOS ONE)

📄 Evaluating a decision aid for mental health disclosure: Insights from a think-aloud & pilot study (Preprint)

📄 Speeding up usability testing: a quick guide to rapid analysis for intervention development (Published in The European Health Psychologist)
